Homemade Red Sauce #3.14

Easy and Delicious Homemade Sauce

There's nothing more comforting than the smell of a pot of sauce simmering on the stove. This can be a simple and fairly quick week night sauce using cans of crushed tomatoes, as this recipe calls for. If you have fresh tomatoes, substitute those for an even more flavorful sauce, following standard directions for reducing those first. This method with canned tomatoes is my everyday-all-year-'round sauce. Feel free to tweak this recipe to your taste. For example, I use a little more oregano for a pizza sauce and usually no oregano for a pasta sauce. I like this amount for a lasagna sauce and just randomly named it with a pi reference.

Ingredients

  • (3) 28 oz. cans crushed tomatoes
  • 14.5 oz. can petite diced tomatoes
  • 6 oz. can tomato paste
  • 1/2 onion -finely diced
  • 3 cloves garlic -crushed and chopped
  • 1/2 tsp. red pepper flakes -or a little more to taste
  • 1/2 tsp. dried oregano
  • 1 tsp. dried basil
  • 2 whole bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp. worcestershire sauce or 1/2 tsp. anchovy paste
  • 1/8 cup fresh chopped parsley
  • 1 tsp. salt
  • 1 tbsp. Olive oil

procedure

Heat oil in skillet and fry onion until soft and slightly carmelized. The sweetness from the onion balances the acidity of the tomatoes. Add garlic and red pepper flakes and fry for another 1/2 minutes. Reduce heat to medium and add diced tomatoes, crushed tomatoes, paste, oregano, basil, bay leaves, and worcestershire. stir occaionally. When sauce starts to bubble reduce heat to low. let sauce simmer while you prepare the rest of dinner or, if making it ahead of time, simmer for 30-60 minutes. Stir occasionally. Turn off heat and stir in parsley and salt.

bon appetit!
