Fresh Salad

Mixed greens salad

This is a simple salad to accompany a meal or to use as a base topped with steak or chicken as a standalone dish.

Ingredients

  • 5 oz. Spring mix
  • 1 bunch Leaf lettuce, chopped
  • 10-12oz Cherry tomatoes
  • ~1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 carrot, shredded
  • splash your favorite salad dressing. I use a simple vinegarette
  • salt and pepper to taste

procedure

Wash veggies and combine.toss with a little dressing. add salt and pepper to individual servings.

bon appetit!
